Job Title: Commercial Heavy Truck Driver (Class A CDL)
Location: Regional Logistics Center
Department: Fleet Transportation and Freight Operations

Job Overview:
We are hiring a dedicated and safety-focused Commercial Heavy Truck Driver to transport freight across regional and interstate distribution corridors. The driver will be responsible for pre-trip inspections, safe vehicle operation, cargo securing, and adherence to Department of Transportation (DOT) safety regulations.

Key Responsibilities:
- Operate heavy tractor-trailers safely across designated regional routes and highway corridors.
- Perform daily pre-trip and post-trip vehicle mechanical inspections (brakes, tires, fluids, coupling systems).
- Secure cargo loads properly using straps, chains, and load locks to prevent transit damage.
- Maintain accurate electronic logging device (ELD) records of duty status and driving hours in compliance with DOT regulations.
- Report mechanical malfunctions, transit delays, or hazardous road conditions promptly to fleet dispatch.

Qualifications and Requirements:
- Valid Commercial Driver's License (CDL Class A) with a clean driving record.
- Minimum 2 years of commercial long-haul or regional freight driving experience.
- Thorough understanding of federal DOT safety rules, hours-of-service (HOS), and weight station regulations.
- Ability to perform basic mechanical troubleshooting, tire chain installation, and minor adjustments.
- Punctual, dependable, and capable of operating independently with high safety awareness.
