Job Title: Clinical Pharmacist
Location: City General Hospital | Medical Sciences Division
Department: Pharmacy and Clinical Therapeutics

Job Overview:
We are seeking a licensed Clinical Pharmacist to oversee patient medication regimens, collaborate with multidisciplinary clinical teams, perform therapeutic drug monitoring, and ensure compliance with pharmaceutical care standards and GMP protocols.

Key Responsibilities:
- Review and evaluate patient prescriptions for appropriate drug selection, dosage calculation, contraindications, and drug-drug interactions.
- Conduct therapeutic drug monitoring (TDM) and pharmacokinetics assessments for specialized inpatient care.
- Compound and dispense sterile intravenous medications following Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) and aseptic standards.
- Provide drug information consultation to physicians, nursing staff, and counsel patients upon hospital discharge.
- Maintain accurate electronic medical records (EMR), track controlled substances, and adhere to hospital pharmacy SOPs.

Qualifications and Requirements:
- Bachelor of Pharmacy (B.Pharm) or Doctor of Pharmacy (Pharm.D) from an accredited institution.
- Active professional pharmacist license / pharmacy council registration.
- 2+ years of hospital or clinical pharmacy experience.
- Strong knowledge of pharmacology, pharmacokinetics, and clinical dosage calculations.
- Experience with aseptic IV compounding, HPLC quality verification, and GMP compliance.
- Excellent patient counseling, interpersonal communication, and clinical documentation abilities.
